Lecturer since 2023 in the [European Doctoral School of Demography](https://eaps.nl/edsd) master's programme. The course covers methods for measuring and interpreting mortality shocks: excess mortality estimation, age-period-cohort analysis, and the demographic consequences of epidemics, conflict and other crises.
