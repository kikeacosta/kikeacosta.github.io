Enrique Acosta is a *Ramón y Cajal* Research Fellow and Established Research Scientist (R3) at the Centre d'Estudis Demogràfics (CED, Barcelona) and an Affiliate Researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research (MPIDR, Rostock). He is a demographer specializing in mortality analysis and the demography of crises, including violence, armed conflict, epidemics, and behavioral causes of death.

======
* **Research Scientist (R3, *Ramón y Cajal*)**, 2022–present. [Centre d'Estudis Demogràfics (CED)](https://ced.cat/directori/enrique-acosta/), Barcelona, Spain.
* **Affiliate Researcher**, 2022–present. [Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research (MPIDR)](https://www.demogr.mpg.de/en/about_us_6113/staff_directory_1899/enrique_acosta_3483/), [Kinship Inequalities Research Group](https://www.demogr.mpg.de/en/research_6120/independent_research_groups_11668/kinship_inequalities_10703/), Rostock, Germany.
